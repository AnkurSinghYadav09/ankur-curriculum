## Topics covered
- Vectors from Scratch : What is a scalar vs a vector. Representing vectors as arrows and as coordinate lists. Vector addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication geometrically and algebraically. Magnitude using the Pythagorean theorem. Unit vectors.
- Dot Product & Angle Between Vectors  : Formula: a·b =
- a
- b
- cos(θ). Computing it by hand. What the result tells you  positive means the same direction, zero means perpendicular, negative means opposite. Cosine of an angle derived from the dot product formula step by step.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this session, learners can:
- Compute vector addition, subtraction and scalar multiplication
- Compute the magnitude of a vector using the L2 norm
- Compute the dot product of two vectors
- Compute the angle between two vectors using the cosine formula
