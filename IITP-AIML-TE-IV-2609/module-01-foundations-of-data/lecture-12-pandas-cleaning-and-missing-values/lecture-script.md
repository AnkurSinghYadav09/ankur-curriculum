## Learning outcomes
By the end of this lecture, learners can:
- Identify missing values disguised as sentinels such as 999, 'NA', 'unknown' or an empty string
- Remove rows or columns with missing values, reporting the row loss caused
- Impute missing numeric values using the column mean or median
- Select between mean and median imputation, justifying by the column's skew and extreme values
- Diagnose the distribution distortion introduced by mean imputation

## Topics to cover
- Builds on 5.1: the loaded orders table has gaps, and a model trained on gaps learns the gaps
- finding missing values, including the ones disguised as sentinels like 999, 'NA' or an empty cell, and seeing how much of each column is absent
- dropping rows or columns, and reporting the row loss that choice costs
- filling instead of dropping, with the mean or the median, why a skewed column prefers the median, and how filling with the mean quietly narrows the spread of the data
