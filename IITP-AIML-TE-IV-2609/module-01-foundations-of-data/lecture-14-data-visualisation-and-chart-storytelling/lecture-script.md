# Lecture Script: Data Visualisation & Chart Storytelling

**Batch:** IITP-AIML-TE-IV-2609

**Week:** 6 | **Session:** 6.2
**Taken by:** Industry Mentor

---

## Learning outcomes
By the end of this lecture, learners can:
- Build a bar chart comparing a metric across categories
- Build a histogram of a continuous variable, choosing a bin width
- Format a chart with a title, axis labels, units and a legend
- Build a Seaborn plot from a long-format DataFrame, mapping columns to x, y and hue
- Select a chart type for a stated question, justifying by the comparison asked for and each variable's measurement type
- Write a chart title that states the takeaway rather than the chart's subject

## Topics to cover
- Builds on 6.1: the cleaned, grouped tables from pandas, SQL and pivots don't persuade on their own, so turn them into charts
- plotting with matplotlib: bar charts and histograms
- titles, axis labels and legends
- seaborn for statistical charts straight from a tidy DataFrame, mapping columns to x, y and hue
- choosing the right chart for the question
- storytelling with takeaway titles
