## Topics covered
- Probability Fundamentals :  Sample space, events, P(A), complement rule, addition rule P(A∪B), multiplication rule for independent events. Classical probability with coins, dice, cards. Conditional probability P(A
- B)  how knowing one thing changes the probability of another.
- Bayes' Theorem from First Principles : Derived step by step from conditional probability. One concrete real-world example worked out by hand (disease test or spam). The idea that prior belief + new evidence = updated belief. Stated as a formula, not a model.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this session, learners can:
- Compute the probability of an event and of its complement
- Apply the addition rule to mutually exclusive and to overlapping events
- Compute a conditional probability from a two-way table
- Explain how Bayes theorem follows from the definition of conditional probability
- Apply Bayes' theorem to update a probability given a test result
- Compute joint and marginal probabilities from a two-way table
