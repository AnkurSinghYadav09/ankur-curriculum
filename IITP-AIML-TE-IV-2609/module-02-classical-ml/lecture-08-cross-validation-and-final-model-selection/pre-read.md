## Topics covered
- Builds on 11.1: several candidate classifiers now exist for the cancellation target (logistic, tree, forest), so compare them fairly on one metric

- scoring models on identical folds
- reading fold-score spread
- metric tables

- saving the chosen model
- explaining the chosen model's predictions and feature importances to a non-technical business audience

## Learning objectives
By the end of this session, learners can:
- Apply k-fold cross-validation to obtain a stable estimate of model performance
- Compute cross-validated scores for two candidate models under an identical fold scheme
- Interpret the spread of cross-validation fold scores as evidence of score stability
- Select a final model from a metric table, justifying by score margin against model complexity
- Write a fitted model to disk as a serialised artifact
- Rewrite a technical finding in language a non-specialist reader can act on
